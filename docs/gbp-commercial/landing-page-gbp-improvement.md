**Title:** Google Business Profile Improvement | MaximisedAI
**Meta description:** A one-off review and update of one verified Google Business Profile, with up to five confirmed services. NZD pricing excluding GST.

## Make your existing Business Profile more accurate and useful

If you control a verified Google Business Profile, we can review its public information, agree the changes that matter and submit those updates. You approve the facts and retain ownership.

## Choose by the number of services you provide

| One-off option | Price |
|---|---:|
| 1–3 agreed services | NZD 199 + GST |
| 4–5 agreed services | NZD 299 + GST |

One option covers one profile for one business location or service-area business. A service is one distinct, real offering listed on that profile; it is not a website page or a search keyword. We confirm the list, access and tier before you commit. The fee is for our defined improvement work; Google Business Profile is available at no charge. There is no subscription.

## What's included

- One review of the live profile against business details you confirm, with a prioritized change list.
- Approved corrections to applicable description, hours, contact details, website link, category and address or service-area settings.
- Addition, correction or organization of the agreed 1–3 or 4–5 genuine service entries, where the profile's available fields permit them.
- One review and correction round, followed by a record of changes submitted and any items awaiting Google review.

## Before we start

You need control of the verified profile and must authorize access for the work. You supply accurate business facts and approve changes. If access, verification, suspension or ownership is unresolved, we first scope that issue separately. Google may delay, modify or reject changes.

## Clear boundaries

This option excludes website work, SEO campaigns, ads, ongoing monitoring, review generation or replies, photos, multiple locations, appeals or reinstatement, duplicate merges and ownership disputes. More than five services or unusual remediation needs a separate written scope. No ranking, traffic or lead outcome is promised.
