**Title:** Google Business Profile Setup | MaximisedAI  
**Meta description:** One-off help setting up one eligible Google Business Profile, with up to five confirmed services. Clear scope and NZD pricing excluding GST.

## Set up a Business Profile your customers can recognise

If your eligible business does not yet control a verified Google Business Profile, we can help create or claim one, enter accurate business details and guide you through Google's verification process. You retain ownership of the profile.

## Choose by the number of services you provide

| One-off option | Price |
|---|---:|
| 1–3 agreed services | NZD 249 + GST |
| 4–5 agreed services | NZD 349 + GST |

One option covers one profile for one business location or service-area business. A service is one distinct, real offering listed on that profile; it is not a website page or a search keyword. We confirm the list and tier before you commit. The fee is for our setup work; Google Business Profile is available at no charge. There is no subscription.

## What's included

- Eligibility and duplicate-profile check, then creation or claim of one profile with your authorization.
- Entry or correction of your supplied name, primary category, contact details, website link where available, business description, hours and applicable address or service-area details.
- Addition or correction of the agreed 1–3 or 4–5 genuine service entries, where the profile's available fields permit them.
- Guidance through the verification method Google offers, including one assistance session.
- One review and correction round, then a handover record of submitted details and any pending Google steps.

## Before we start

You provide accurate business details and service descriptions, approve the content, retain profile ownership and take part in verification. If someone else controls an existing profile, ownership must be resolved before this fixed-price setup can proceed. Google chooses the verification methods and reviews profile changes; we cannot guarantee when a profile or edit will appear.

## Clear boundaries

This option excludes website creation, SEO campaigns, ads, ongoing management, review generation, photos, multiple locations, appeals or reinstatement, duplicate merges and ownership disputes. More than five services or unusual remediation needs a separate written scope. No ranking, traffic or lead outcome is promised.
